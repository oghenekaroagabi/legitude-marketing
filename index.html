The Problem

The journal isn't working anymore

Nigerian private school bursars are doing their best with physical books and WhatsApp screenshots. But directors are flying blind — and mistakes are costing schools money every term.

No visibility for directors

The director has to ask the bursar and wait. By the time he gets a number, it's already outdated.

Defaulters slip through

Tracking who hasn't paid across hundreds of students in a physical book takes hours — and things still get missed.

Receipt disputes

Parents claim they paid. The bursar can't find the record. Nobody has proof. It becomes a conversation nobody wants.

Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Finance-only focus. No attendance tracking, no report cards, no virtual classrooms. Just school fee management done properly — built around how Nigerian schools actually work.

Journal-style payment recording

The bursar records payments the same way she always has. Student, amount, fee type, payment method. No retraining required.

Director dashboard

The director sees how much has been collected, what's outstanding, and who hasn't paid — updated every time a payment is recorded.

Defaulter tracking

Instantly see which students owe fees, how much, and for which fee type. No manual cross-checking at end of term.

Class-based fee structures

Different fees for different classes. School fees, book fees, uniform fees — each handled separately with the right logic per item.

Flash drive data export

Export all payment records to CSV at any time. Save to a flash drive, keep a backup, share with anyone. Your data is always yours.

Works offline

No internet? No problem. Legitude runs on any school laptop without needing a constant connection. Built for Nigerian reality.
